Backup Your Shop Data

Create a complete backup of your shop data to ensure it is safe and can be restored if needed. When the backup is ready, you can download it directly from this page or receive a link in your email notification.

The following items will be included:

  • All products and product categories
  • All customer data and profiles
  • All sales transactions and invoices
  • All subscriptions, plans, and module settings
  • All shop configurations and settings
  • All uploaded media and attachments

The following items will NOT be included:

  • Payment gateway secrets and API keys
  • Session data and temporary cache files
  • System logs
  • Encrypted tokens or credentials
  • Third-party integrations (they must be reconfigured manually)

Delete Store

This will permanently remove all store-related data including products, categories, orders, and subscriptions. Your account will remain active, and you can create a new store later.

Delete Account

Deleting your account will permanently remove your account and all associated stores. You will no longer be able to log in or recover any data. This action is irreversible.

Deleting an account has the following effects:

  • All store data, including products, categories, orders, and subscriptions, will be permanently deleted.
  • All backups linked to the account will be deleted.
