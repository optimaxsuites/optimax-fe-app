What Each Employee Card Shows

  • Department badge — color-coded label at the top of the card
  • Status indicator — pulsing green (Active) or amber dot (Away)
  • Profile photo — employee avatar or default placeholder
  • Full name — Last name + First name
  • Job title — e.g., "Senior Developer", "Marketing Lead"
  • Work email — official email address
  • Department & Employment type chips
  • Organizational level & contract type
  • Date joined — hire date
  • Employee ID — system identifier
  • Staff ID button — opens printable QR-coded identity card
  • View Profile button — opens full editable employee record

Filtering & Searching

  1. Filter by Level Type — Multi-select dropdown to show employees of specific organizational levels.
  2. Filter by Position — Multi-select to narrow by specific job titles.
  3. Search by Name or Title — Live search field — grid filters instantly as you type.

Adding a New Employee

  1. Click "Add new employee" in the toolbar. A side panel slides in from the right.
  2. Personal Information — Name, DOB, gender, phone, email, address.
  3. Employment Details — Job title, department, level, employment type, hire date.
  4. Salary Structure — Base salary, allowances, bonuses, deductions, overtime.
  5. Financial Information — Bank name, account number, BVN, NIN.
  6. Next of Kin — Emergency contact details.
  7. Save — Employee is immediately available for payroll, leave, and attendance.

Digital Staff ID Card

Click Staff ID on any employee card to generate a printable identity card with:

  • Company logo and name
  • Employee photo, name, job title, department, and level
  • Staff ID, work email, phone, and date joined
  • QR code and barcode for HR scanning
  • Reverse side with emergency contact and verification policy
  • Download PDF button

Vacancies

Hiring Workflow

  1. Create a Vacancy — Position title, department, salary range, description, requirements, deadline.
  2. Post as Job Opening — Publish to external job board for candidates to find and apply.
  3. Receive Applications — All applications appear in the Candidates tab automatically.
  4. Screen Candidates — Review, shortlist, and move candidates to interview stage.
  5. Schedule Interviews — Assign date, time, and interviewer. Auto-sends email invitation to candidate.
  6. Score the Interview — Digital score sheet rates candidates across defined criteria.
  7. Send Offer Letter — Generate branded offer letter with salary details directly from system.
  8. Send Appointment Letter — Once offer accepted, generate formal appointment letter.
  9. Onboard Employee — New hire moves into the Employee Management module automatically.
✅
Key Feature for SalesOffer letters and appointment letters are auto-generated with company branding. Eliminates manual document creation and ensures consistency across all hires.

Leave Requests (Approval Workflow)

HR admins and line managers review pending requests. For each request they can:

  • View requester name, leave type, date range, and reason
  • Check employee's current leave balance
  • Approve — marks approved and deducts from balance
  • Reject — declines with optional reason

Leave Policy

Admins define: leave types (Annual, Medical, Maternity, Paternity, etc.), days allocated per type per year, carry-over rules, and notice period requirements.

Payroll Components

ComponentTypeDescription
Base SalaryEarningsFixed monthly salary from salary structure

Net PayResultGross Earnings minus Total Deductions

Multi-Stage Approval Workflow

  1. HR Prepares Payroll — Reviews entries, confirms attendance & loan deductions.
  2. Stage 1 Approval — HR Manager reviews and approves or rejects.
  3. Stage 2 Approval — Finance Director gives final sign-off.
  4. Payroll Locked — Locked after approval; payslips become available to employees.
✅
Key Selling PointMulti-stage approval prevents unauthorized payroll changes — critical compliance and audit requirement for regulated industries.

Loans & Contributions

Navigate to HR → Contributions and Loans — employee loans plus full cooperative management.

Employee Loan Workflow

  1. Employee Submits Request — Selects loan purpose, amount, and repayment period.
  2. HR Reviews Eligibility — Checks salary, existing loans, and tenure.
  3. Loan Approval/Rejection — Authorized approver signs off with optional note.
  4. Disbursement to Bank — Approved loan transferred to employee account.
  5. Auto Payroll Deduction — Monthly repayments automatically deducted until settled.

Common Objections & Responses

ObjectionResponse
"We already have a payroll system"OptimaX integrates payroll with attendance, leave, and loans — one source of truth. No more import/export between tools.
"Our HR needs are simple"Start with what you need today and activate more features as you grow — no system migration required.
"Is it hard to learn?"Most features work in under 3 clicks. Designed for non-technical HR staff. Full training and documentation included.
"How secure is our data?"Role-based access — employees only see their own data. Salary and BVN protected. All data encrypted at rest and in transit.
"Does it work for multiple branches?"Yes — multi-workspace support. Each branch operates independently; HQ gets consolidated reporting.

Recommended Demo Flow

  1. Employee Directory — Show the card grid, filter by department, click Staff ID to show the QR-coded ID card. Visual impact is immediate.
  2. Payroll Processing — Display approval tracker and a payslip. Emphasize multi-stage approval and auto deductions.
  3. Leave Management — Submit a leave request as employee, switch to admin and approve. Show balance update in real time.
  4. Recruitment Pipeline — Create a vacancy, show candidate pipeline, open offer letter template.
  5. Attendance & Shifts — Show attendance sheet with clock-in/out times. If prospect has shift workers, demo the Shift Calendar.
