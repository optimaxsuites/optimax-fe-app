Dashboard, Expense, and HR Attendance Native App Review

This deck proposes a mobile-first, enterprise-grade product direction for the highest-frequency employee workflows: visibility, expense submission, and attendance action. The target is a calm, native-feeling experience that improves speed without losing operational clarity.

Focus Modules Dashboard, Expense, HR Attendance
Primary Users Employees, managers, executives
Recommended Direction Concept 2 with select traits from 1 and 3
Phase 1 Priorities
  • Fast clock in and clock out with immediate state confirmation
  • Guided receipt-to-claim expense flow with policy awareness
  • Role-aware dashboard with pending approvals and business health snapshot
  • Unified bottom navigation with clear task ownership
Selection Guidance
  • Choose Concept 1 if executive polish is the main driver
  • Choose Concept 2 if broad staff adoption is the main driver
  • Choose Concept 3 if operational depth is the main driver
  • Blend them if the final app must support all three audiences strongly

PRD source: docs/native-app-prototype/prd.md

Concept 1

Executive Clarity

Best for leadership visibility and premium KPI storytelling. The layout foregrounds confidence, summary insight, and clean decision points.

Best Use Executive dashboarding, manager approvals, polished first impression
Strengths High-trust look, balanced density, crisp status cues, strong boardroom appeal
Tradeoff Slightly less guided for first-time or process-heavy users than Concept 2

Recommended when stakeholder confidence and a premium leadership view are more important than step-by-step operational handholding.

Concept 2

Guided Momentum

Best for broad employee adoption. This direction emphasizes next actions, guided flows, and task completion with a friendlier enterprise tone.

Best Use Daily staff workflows, fast onboarding, scalable adoption across teams
Strengths Clear action hierarchy, guided expense submission, approachable attendance flow
Tradeoff Slightly less analytical and executive in tone than Concepts 1 and 3

This is the best default foundation for the first release because it keeps users moving without dumbing the product down.

Concept 3

Precision Workspace

Best for operations-heavy teams that need denser information, sharper controls, and more structured drill-down behavior.

Best Use Managers, operational leads, exception handling, detail review
Strengths High information yield, stronger policy and exception framing, richer analytics cues
Tradeoff Heavier cognitive load for casual or infrequent users

Recommended as a source of patterns for manager views and advanced screens, even if the overall product tone follows Concept 2.
