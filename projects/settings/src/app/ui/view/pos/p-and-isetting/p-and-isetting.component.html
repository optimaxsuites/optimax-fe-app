Paystack Payment Account Setup (6 Steps)

Step 1: Create a Paystack Account

👉 Click here to sign up on Paystack

  • Enter your email, password, and business details.
  • Verify your email address.
Step 2: Complete Business Verification (KYC)
  • In your Paystack dashboard, go to Settings → Compliance / KYC.
  • Upload the required documents:
    • Individuals → Govt. ID + Proof of Address + Bank details.
    • Registered Businesses → CAC documents, Directors’ ID, Proof of Address.
  • Wait for Paystack to approve your account (usually 1–3 business days).
  • Without verification, you cannot access live API keys.
Step 3: Add Bank Account for Settlements
  • In dashboard, go to Settings → Payouts / Bank Accounts.
  • Add your bank name + account number.
  • Funds from customer payments will be settled into this bank account.
Step 4: Get Your API Keys
  • Log in to your dashboard: https://dashboard.paystack.com
  • Go to Settings → API Keys & Webhooks.
  • Copy your Live Public Key and Live Secret Key.
  • ⚠️ Important: Never share your Secret Key publicly. Only paste it inside this ERP system.
Step 5: Paste API Keys Into This ERP

Return to your ERP system’s Payment Settings page and paste the keys below:

Step 6: Verify Connection
  • Our ERP will test your Paystack keys upon saving.
  • If valid, you’ll see a “Connected Successfully” message.
  • Done! All payments will now be processed via Paystack. 🎉
⚠️ Note for Users:
  • Test Keys are only for trial and will not send money to your bank.
