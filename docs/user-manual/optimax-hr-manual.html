📘 Chapter 1

What is OptimaX?

A full-suite Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform built for modern businesses.

OptimaX is a comprehensive, cloud-based ERP system that unifies all major business operations — from Human Resources and Payroll to Sales, CRM, Inventory, Accounting, and more — into a single, seamless platform. It is designed for growing businesses that need professional-grade tools without the complexity of legacy enterprise software.

OptimaX Modules at a Glance

💳

📊

🤝

📦

🛒

💡
Sales Talking Point OptimaX replaces 6–10 disconnected software tools with one integrated platform, reducing IT overhead and giving management real-time visibility across every department.

Why the HR Module Stands Out

  • End-to-end coverage — from job posting to retirement, every HR operation is handled in one place.
  • Role-based access — employees see only what they need; managers and HR have full control.
  • Real-time data — attendance, leave balances, and payroll figures update live.
  • Integrated payroll — salary, deductions, loans, bonuses, and tax calculations are all automated.
  • Mobile-responsive — works on desktops, tablets, and smartphones without a separate app.
  • Digital ID cards — generate QR-coded staff identity cards with one click.

Filtering & Searching Employees

Three filter controls appear in the toolbar above the card grid:

  1. Filter by Level Type — Multi-select dropdown to show employees of specific organizational levels (e.g., Senior, Junior, Management).
  2. Filter by Position — Multi-select dropdown to narrow by specific job titles or roles.
  3. Search by Name or Title — Type any part of an employee's name or job title for instant live filtering.
💡
Sales Demo Tip Demonstrate the real-time search: type a name and watch the grid filter instantly — no page reload. This resonates with HR managers who spend time scrolling through long employee lists.

Adding a New Employee

  1. Click "Add new employee" — Located in the toolbar above the employee grid. A full-screen side panel slides in from the right.
  2. Fill in Personal Information — First name, last name, date of birth, gender, phone number, email address, home address.
  3. Set Employment Details — Job title, department, organizational level, employment type (Full-time/Part-time/Contract), hire date.
  4. Configure Salary Structure — Base salary, allowances (transport, meal, housing), bonuses, deductions, and overtime settings.
  5. Add Financial Information — Bank name, account number, BVN, NIN for payroll processing.
  6. Add Next of Kin — Emergency contact details and relationship.
  7. Save the Record — Click Save. The employee is immediately added to the directory and is ready for payroll, leave, and attendance tracking.

Step-by-Step Hiring Workflow

  1. Create a Vacancy — Navigate to the Vacancies tab and click "Create Vacancy". Fill in the position title, department, salary range, job description, requirements, and application deadline.
  2. Post as a Job Opening — After creating a vacancy, publish it as a Job Opening. This makes it visible to applicants on the public-facing job board.
  3. Receive Applications — Candidates apply through the external job portal. All applications appear in the Candidates tab with applicant details and resume links.
  4. Screen Candidates — Review applications, shortlist promising candidates, and move them to the interview stage.
  5. Schedule Interviews — Assign an interview date, time, and interviewer. The system sends an automated interview invitation email to the candidate.
  6. Score the Interview — Use the digital score sheet to rate candidates across defined criteria during or after the interview.
  7. Send Offer Letter — For successful candidates, generate and send a formal offer letter with salary details directly from the system.
  8. Send Appointment Letter — Once the offer is accepted, generate the appointment letter to formalize the hire.
  9. Onboard the Employee — The newly hired candidate is onboarded into the employee management module automatically.

Payroll Approval Workflow

OptimaX includes a multi-stage payroll approval process to ensure accuracy and compliance:

  1. HR prepares payroll — Reviews all entries for the month, confirms attendance and loan deductions are applied.
  2. Stage 1 Approval — First approver (e.g., HR Manager) reviews and approves or rejects.
  3. Stage 2 Approval — Second approver (e.g., Finance Director) gives final sign-off.
  4. Payroll Processed — Once fully approved, payroll is locked and payslips become available to employees.
✅
Key Selling Point The multi-stage approval workflow prevents unauthorized payroll changes — a critical compliance and audit requirement for regulated industries and growing businesses.

Payslips

After payroll is approved, each employee can view and download their payslip. Navigate to HR → Payroll and click View Payslips or use the Actions menu. Payslips show a full itemized breakdown of earnings and deductions for the period.

Employee Loan Workflow

  1. Employee Submits Loan Request — Selects loan purpose, amount, and preferred repayment period.
  2. HR Reviews Request — Reviews employee eligibility (salary, existing loans, tenure).
  3. Loan Approval — Authorized approver approves or rejects. Rejection includes a reason.
  4. Loan Disbursement — Approved loan is disbursed to the employee's bank account.
  5. Automatic Deductions — Monthly repayment amounts are automatically deducted from payroll until the loan is settled.

Common Objections & Responses

ObjectionResponse
"We already have a payroll system" OptimaX's payroll is integrated with attendance, leave, and loans — eliminating the need to export/import data between tools. You get one source of truth.
"Our HR is simple, we don't need all this" You start with what you need today (e.g., employee directory + payroll) and activate features as you grow — without switching systems.
"Is it hard to learn?" The interface is designed for non-technical HR staff. Most features work in under 3 clicks. We also provide onboarding training and documentation.
"How secure is our employee data?" Role-based access means employees only see their own data. Sensitive fields like salary and BVN are protected. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
"Can it handle multiple branches/offices?" Yes — OptimaX supports multi-workspace environments. Each branch can have its own workspace, with consolidated reporting for headquarters.
