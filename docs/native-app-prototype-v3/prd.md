# Native App PRD V3

## Product
OptimaXSuites Native App

## Objective
Design a native-feeling phone and tablet experience for the full OptimaXSuites platform, using real database names supplied by the client and presenting the product as one connected enterprise suite rather than a narrow module-specific app.

## Scope
This revision expands beyond Dashboard, Expenses, HR Attendance, and Collaboration. The prototype direction now treats the suite as a broad enterprise platform including:
- Dashboard
- Collaboration
- Expenses
- HR
- Attendance
- Payroll
- Leave
- Procurement
- Inventory
- Accounting
- POS
- CRM
- Reports
- Settings

## Core Design Requirements
- Use OptimaXSuites naming, not generic product naming.
- Use real database-provided employee names in visible UI surfaces.
- Use Nigerian Naira as the default currency in all financial screens.
- Show both phone and tablet layouts.
- Keep the product enterprise, operational, and rollout-ready.
- Reflect a multi-module suite with connected navigation, approvals, reporting, and collaboration.

## Experience Goals
- Give leadership a full-suite executive snapshot.
- Give operations and finance teams fast access to expenses, approvals, procurement, and reporting.
- Give workforce teams easy access to attendance, leave, payroll, and HR actions.
- Give all users a connected collaboration surface across modules.

## Device Strategy

### Tablet
- multi-column workspace layouts
- left navigation across all modules
- denser reporting, approvals, collaboration, and workforce views
- better scanability for managers and executives

### Phone
- compact dashboard and approvals
- quick expense submission and approval review
- attendance check-in and leave summary
- collaboration feed, direct messages, and alerts
- fast cross-module navigation with bottom tabs

## Functional Themes

### Unified Dashboard
- top KPIs
- approvals queue
- cross-module alerts
- expense and payroll summaries
- procurement and inventory alerts
- employee-driven activity stream

### Finance and Expense
- Naira-based totals
- approval counts
- employee expense tables
- expense submission
- reimbursement monitoring
- analytics and imports

### HR and Workforce
- attendance status
- leave balance
- payroll summaries
- correction requests
- team attendance
- open positions and hiring cues

### Collaboration
- enterprise feed
- channels
- direct messages
- pinned files
- task reminders
- cross-module quick links

## V3 Concept Summary
- Concept 1, `OptimaXSuites Unified Operations`: best overall full-suite story and strongest platform balance.
- Concept 2, `OptimaXSuites Workflow Matrix`: best departmental workflow clarity and strongest HR/Expense operations blend.
- Concept 3, `OptimaXSuites Enterprise Grid`: best executive, governance, and data-rich rollout story.

## Recommendation
Use Concept 1 as the primary direction. It gives the clearest “one suite, every function” message while still showing credible phone and tablet behavior. Pull selective ideas from Concept 2 for workflow clarity and Concept 3 for executive reporting density.
