1. HR Module Overview

OptimaX HR centralizes employee records, recruitment, attendance, leave, payroll, loans, and performance into a single operational workspace. For prospects, the value is reduced fragmentation, faster approvals, better audit visibility, and stronger employee lifecycle control.

Employee Lifecycle

From recruitment and onboarding to payroll, performance, and offboarding in one module.

Process Visibility

Approvals, requests, attendance events, payslips, and HR documents are easier to track and demonstrate.

Cross-Team Value

HR, finance, and operations align better because the module connects workforce actions to real business workflows.

Sales angle Position the HR module as an operational control center, not just an employee directory.

2. HR Navigation and Layout

Prospects should quickly understand where major HR work happens. Use this section to explain the top shell, the left sidebar, and the functional grouping of HR pages.

What to point out in a demo
  1. Open the HR module from the main enterprise navigation.
  2. Show the sidebar categories for employee, recruitment, attendance, leave, payroll, performance, and loans.
  3. Explain that most pages follow a predictable pattern: summary, list view, filters, and action modal or form.

Suggested talk track

  • “Users do not need to jump between separate HR tools.”
  • “Navigation is organized around real HR responsibilities.”
  • “The same layout pattern shortens training time.”

3. Employee Management

Employee Management is the foundational HR story. This is where sales teams should show how organizations manage staff records, profile data, onboarding-related entries, and structured employee visibility.

Employee Directory

List and browse employees with core profile and employment information.

Employee Creation

Add employee records and collect structured data for HR operations.

Profile Enrichment

Capture guarantor, next of kin, bank, identification, and work details as records mature.

Demo flow
  1. Open the employee list and show search or browsing behavior.
  2. Open an employee record and explain the depth of information stored.
  3. Open the add employee flow to show how new staff can be entered into the system.

10. Sales Demo Playbook

Use this sequence when presenting the HR module to a prospect so the story remains simple, credible, and business-led.

  1. Start with the HR overview and show that the module covers the entire workforce lifecycle.
  2. Move into Employee Management to establish the system of record.
  3. Show Recruitment to position operational control before employment starts.
  4. Show Attendance and Leave to prove daily workforce administration value.
  5. Show Payroll for trust, visibility, and business-critical outcomes.
  6. Show Loans and Performance only if the buyer profile matches those needs.
Recommended demo message “OptimaX HR is not just for storing employee data. It gives organizations one operational workspace to recruit, manage, pay, monitor, and support their workforce.”
